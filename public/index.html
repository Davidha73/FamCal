JustUs Help & Guide

Everything you need to know about your family app
calendar_month

Calendar & Events

View and manage the whole family's schedule in one shared place.

  • Add Events: Tap any date on the calendar, then tap + Add Event. Fill in the event title, start and finish times, category, and who it's for.
  • Month & Week Views: Use the segmented toggle at the top of the calendar to switch between the full month view and an expanded week view.
  • Work Shifts: Set up your standard work shifts (Early, Late, Night) under Default Shifts in Settings. You can quickly stamp shifts onto calendar days with one tap.
  • Jump to Today: Tap the calendar icon in the top header bar anytime to jump back to today's date.
sticky_note_2

Noticeboard: Notes & Lists

The family noticeboard keeps sticky notes, reminders, and live shared checklists organized.

  • Pin a Note: Tap + Note to write a note, reminder, or shoutout for the family. You can choose a category icon and pin important notes to stay at the top.
  • Shared Checklists: Tap + List to create packing lists, chore lists, or grocery lists. Anyone in the family can tap items to check them off in real-time.
  • Link a List to a Note: When creating a checklist, you can optionally link it directly to an existing note card.
  • Filter Chips: Use the filter pills at the top to filter items by category (Work, Tasks, Fun, Family) or by family member.
forum

Messages & Family Chat

Stay in touch instantly with dedicated real-time family messaging.

  • Unread Badges: The Messages tab badge alerts you whenever new messages arrive from other family members.
  • Message Actions: Tap the convert icon on any message bubble to instantly turn that message into a calendar event, note, or checklist.
alternate_email

The '@' Mention System

Tag specific family members in messages or notes so they never miss important updates.

  • How to Use: Type @ in the message input or in a note description. A suggestion bar will appear with Dad, Mum, Owen, and Liv.
  • Signature Color Badges: Tapping a family member inserts a pill styled in their unique member color.
  • Priority Push Alerts: When someone tags you with @YourName, your device receives a targeted alert notification highlighting that you were mentioned!
terminal

The '/' Slash Shortcuts & Conversions

Quickly turn chat messages into actionable items using slash commands.

  • /note [text] – Instantly opens a new note with your text prefilled.
  • /list [title] – Creates a new checklist with your text as the list title.
  • /event [title] – Opens the calendar event form with your text ready as the event summary.
  • /link – Shows your active notes and checklists so you can attach a card link to your chat message.
  • Copy Existing Messages: Tap the transfer button (drive_file_move) on any sent message bubble to copy its text directly into a note, list, or event without retyping.
tune

Settings & Push Alerts

Configure JustUs to match your preferences.

  • Appearance: Toggle between Dark Mode and Light Mode anytime.
  • Who Are You: Choose your default family profile so new events and messages automatically attribute to you.
  • Push Alerts: Enable Push Alerts to receive real-time notifications even when the app is in the background. On iOS, add JustUs to your Home Screen to enable notifications.
  • Offline Support: JustUs caches your schedules and notes so you can view your family calendar even with no mobile signal.
